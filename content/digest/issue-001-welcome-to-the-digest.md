This is a **sample issue**. It exists so you can see how a digest issue looks and so the
build has something to render. Delete it when the first real issue is ready
(task M1-DIG-04).

## Top story

One or two paragraphs on the most important thing that happened in quantum this week.
Say what happened, why it matters, and what is still unproven.

## Explain it simply

Pick one idea from the week and explain it to a first-year student with no physics
background. No jargon without a one-line definition.

## Papers to skim

- A paper title, with one sentence on why it is interesting.

## India corner

Anything quantum that happened in India this week.

## One thing to try

A small, concrete thing a reader can do in 30 minutes, such as running a circuit in Qiskit.

cut it.
