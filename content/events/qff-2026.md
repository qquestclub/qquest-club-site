Qiskit Fall Fest is a series of student-run quantum computing events supported by IBM,
held each fall at universities worldwide. This edition is organised by QQuest and is open
to students from any college.

Two sessions: **Quantum 101** (a talk, no prerequisites) and **Hands-on with Qiskit** (a
workshop, bring a laptop and know basic Python).

Full schedule, FAQ and checklist are on the event site.
